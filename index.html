About

I hunt vulnerabilities in two places that rarely share an audience: smart contracts holding real money, and the web applications everyone trusts by default.

Most writeups stop at what the bug was. I try to write down the part that actually took the time, which is how it was found. The wrong turns, the thing that looked like a bug and was not, and the proof that finally held up.

Private entries above are real credits under programs that do not permit disclosure. They are counted, never described.
